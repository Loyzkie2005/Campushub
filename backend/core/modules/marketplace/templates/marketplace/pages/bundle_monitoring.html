How to read association evidence
Antecedent (IF) and Consequent (THEN)
The conditional and associated products in a directional rule. An association does not establish causation or purchase sequence.
Support
Baskets containing both products ÷ all eligible analyzed baskets, expressed as a percentage.
Confidence
Baskets containing both products ÷ baskets containing the antecedent, expressed as a percentage.
Lift
Confidence ÷ the consequent's share of analyzed baskets. It compares the association with independent purchasing.
Supporting Basket Count
Distinct analyzed baskets containing the entire combination. Quantities and bundle recipes do not create independent co-purchases.

Metrics must use the original analyzed dataset and its denominator. Current settings are not evidence of historical thresholds. Real orders currently record one purchased product per order, without a verified shared multi-product checkout identifier. Synthetic research is kept separate from live seller recommendations.
